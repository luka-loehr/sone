# Transition Demo Credits & Licensing

The three rendered transition demo videos in `assets/demos/` combine full-length Creative Commons vocal recordings from Jamendo and are distributed under **[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)** (tracks licensed under CC BY 3.0 combined with CC BY-SA 3.0 carry CC BY-SA 3.0 on the adapted mix). Each mix is produced by the unmodified Sone pipeline: tempo alignment, four-stem boundary separation, and stem-level crossfading across the ~24 s transition window while streaming gain-matched original stereo masters bit-for-bit outside `[u0, u1]`.
