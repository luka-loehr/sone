\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{\sone{}} (\op{Sone-6.5M}, \op{Sone-v2-Base}, \op{Sone-Pro}, and \op{Sone-Ultra}), a unified four-tier family of neuro-symbolic automatic DJ models designed for single-core CPU, multi-core studio, and WebAssembly deployment. By pairing $4096$-FFT $64$- to $83$-band Dual-Axis phase-coherent separation distilled from a two-stage \op{BS-RoFormer-1297} + \op{HTDemucs-ft} cascade (\op{Cochlea}), vinyl-speed equivariant seven-head perception and comb-grid tracking (\op{Tympanum}), sharp-cut beat-synchronous planning (\op{Cortex v4}), and 12-mode counterfactual defect reranking (\op{Cingulate v4}) with Newton drum-slope alignment ($\le 2.2$\,ms drift) and untouched master stereo splicing, all four tiers execute faster than real-time on commodity CPU cores ($4.8\%$--$47.7\%$ single-core utilisation per song) from one zero-copy \op{model.safetensors} file. Scaling across four NVIDIA Tensor Core GPU generations and two rounds of blind listening demonstrated that studio-grade automatic mixing depends equally on vocal-formant phase fidelity and discrete metrical-psychoacoustic handoff invariants enforced by construction.
