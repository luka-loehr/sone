\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sone{} (Sone-6.5M, Sone-v2-Base, Sone-Pro, and Sone-Ultra), a four-tier family of neuro-symbolic automatic DJ models built for single-core CPU, multi-core, and WebAssembly deployment. By running four compact neural organs strictly inside the $\sim 24$\,s track boundary and splicing untouched master stereo outside $[u_0, u_1]$, all four tiers fit inside a single zero-copy \op{model.safetensors} file ($13.12$--$71.12$\,MB FP16) and render transitions within $4.8\%$--$36.4\%$ of one cloud CPU core per $3$\,min song.

Pairing $4096$-FFT $64$-band Dual-Axis separation distilled from \op{BS-RoFormer-1297} and \op{HTDemucs-ft} (\op{Cochlea}), vinyl-speed equivariant structural perception (\op{Tympanum}), sharp-cut beat-synchronous planning (\op{Cortex v4}), and 12-mode counterfactual defect reranking (\op{Cingulate v4}) with Newton drum-slope alignment ($\le 0.73$\,ms drift across $10$ renders, $0.29$\,ms mean), sustained-singing onset filtering, and gated $260$--$6200$\,Hz zero-phase mid-formant vocal bridging resolved every defect flagged across two rounds of blind listening. Future work includes multi-listener perceptual testing, larger key-annotated corpora for \op{Tympanum-Pro}, non-$4/4$ meters, and further closing the vocal-reverb separation gap within sub-core CPU budgets.
