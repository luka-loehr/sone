\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Datasets and 2-stage teacher distillation.}
Table~\ref{tab:datasets} summarises the training, two-stage GPU teacher-distillation, validation, and human-listening splits across all four organs:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Beat-synchronous transition corpus (\op{Cortex v4} \& \op{Cingulate v4}):} Synthesised by \op{sone-sim} from $60$ beat- and stem-analysed pop, hip-hop, R\&B, and electronic tracks into $26{,}904$ training transitions and $5{,}932$ strictly held-out validation transitions (\op{val.jsonl.gz}, disjoint song pairs), alongside $152$ human-audited transitions ($106$ accepted, $46$ rejected across $30$ defect tags, incorporating all $10$ blind ratings from our \op{Sone-Pro} studio audit in \op{owner.jsonl}).
  \item \textbf{Multi-corpus \& 2-stage \op{BS-RoFormer-1297} distillation (\op{Cochlea}):} Combines $150$ uncompressed $44.1$\,kHz stereo studio multitracks from MUSDB18-HQ~\citep{MUSDB18HQ} ($138$ train, $12$ held-out test) with $12$ leak-free broadcast multitracks ($6$ train, $6$ strictly unseen test songs), $1{,}101$ Stage-1 \op{HTDemucs-ft}~\citep{rouard2023hybrid} Creative Commons $48$\,s 4-stem tracks, and \textbf{$372$ two-stage \op{BS-RoFormer-1297}~\citep{lu2024bsroformer} ($12.98$\,dB vocal SDR) + \op{HTDemucs-ft} $48$\,s 4-stem tracks} ($180$ shard-0 tracks warm-starting \op{Cochlea-Pro v2} from its $7.84$\,dB checkpoint; $192$ disjoint shard-4 tracks training $29.18$\,M Net2Net-expanded \op{Cochlea-Ultra}) streamed from byte-deduplicated MTG-Jamendo~\citep{bogdanov2019mtg} and Song Describer~\citep{manco2023song} audio. Dynamic stem remixing ($65\%$ cross-song mixing, $\pm 6.5$\,dB gain jitter, $20\%$ vocal drop) operates purely in ephemeral Colab RAM with zero retained audio.
  \item \textbf{600-track stem-isolated \& equivariant structure (\op{Tympanum}):} Combines $12$ full seven-head broadcast tracks ($9$ train, $3$ held-out), $150$ MUSDB18-HQ multitracks ($134$ train, $16$ held-out) annotated directly from isolated studio stems, and $450$ GPU-distilled Creative Commons tracks ($584$ training songs total), augmented continuously via Eq.~\eqref{eq:vinyl}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Four-generation GPU fleet and single-core CPU host.}
Table~\ref{tab:hardware} documents every accelerator used across our experiments: NVIDIA Tesla T4 (Turing \op{sm\_75}, $16$\,GB, hardware FP16 Tensor Cores + \op{GradScaler} for vectorized \op{Cortex/Cingulate v4}), dual concurrent NVIDIA L4 GPUs (Ada Lovelace \op{sm\_89}, $24$\,GB, BF16 FlashAttention for \op{BS-RoFormer-1297} distillation, \op{Cochlea-Ultra}, and \op{Cochlea-Pro v2}), NVIDIA A100-SXM4 (Ampere \op{sm\_80}, $40$\,GB), and dual NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs (\op{G4}, \op{sm\_120}, $96$\,GB GDDR7, $81.7$\,GB peak VRAM at $44.8$ six-second stereo examples/s). All production latency, numerical parity, and end-to-end mix renders were executed single-threaded in release-mode Rust (\op{rustc 1.99.1 -C opt-level=3}) on an Intel Xeon VM (Model~151; AVX2, FMA, F16C; $3.8$\,GiB RAM).

\paragraph{Evaluation protocol.}
We report recipe accuracy, bar-offset MAE, and 8-lane MAE for \op{Cortex}; acceptability accuracy and false-positive counts for \op{Cingulate}; $60$\,s MUSDB18-HQ SDR~\citep{vincent2006performance}, MDX23 $1$\,s active-chunk median SDR~\citep{fabbro2024sdx}, vocal-to-instrumental bleed, mixture conservation error, and FP32-vs-FP16 waveform deviation for \op{Cochlea}; $50$\,Hz F1 ($\pm 70$\,ms), voice accuracy, $24$-key accuracy, and tuning MAE for \op{Tympanum}; and short-time $1$\,s RMS loudness floors ($\min \mathrm{RMS}_{1\text{s}}$), beat drift/lag (ms), and blind creator listening judgements on rendered transitions.
