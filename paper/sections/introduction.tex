\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A continuous music radio station or automated DJ player joins each outgoing track (Deck~A) to an incoming track (Deck~B) across a transition zone of four to sixteen bars. Executing a studio-grade transition requires aligning beats and downbeats to sub-$3$\,ms accuracy even on live-drummed tracks, matching tempo without shifting pitch, choosing an entry structure suited to both arrangements (such as layering Deck~B's opening vocals early over Deck~A's instrumental outro or sneaking percussion under a verse), carving conflicting basslines and mid-range chords, muting stem bleed prior to real vocal onsets, and cutting outgoing vocals on a natural breath with a tempo-synced echo tail. Crucially, DJ mixing is a \emph{boundary} operation: for the remaining $85$--$90\%$ of every song outside the overlap $[u_0, u_1]$, listeners expect to hear the original studio master completely unaltered.

End-to-end generative audio models violate this structure in three ways. First, routing full songs through neural waveform or latent codecs degrades non-transition audio and introduces phase-scrambled, watery vocal artifacts on dense mixes. Second, generative audio backbones require hundreds of millions of parameters and dedicated GPUs, whereas streaming platforms serving thousands of concurrent personalised stations require transitions to render asynchronously on fractional cloud vCPUs or client-side WebAssembly workers. Third, DJ mixing is governed by discrete metrical and psychoacoustic invariants---bar phase, sixteenth-note vocal onset locks, relative minor/major Camelot equivalence, and post-drop unity stem continuity---where small regression smoothing errors create jarring perceptual defects even when average $L_1$ loss is low.

We take the opposite approach: \emph{perceive and plan with compact anatomical neural organs inside the boundary window; render and splice master audio with deterministic signal processing}. Outside $[u_0, u_1]$, \sone{} splices the untouched stereo master via raised-cosine seams and smooth post-handoff tempo glides, guaranteeing zero neural coloration on the body of every song and reducing per-track separation compute by $\sim 7.5\times$. Within the boundary window, four specialised neural organs packaged inside one unified \op{model.safetensors} checkpoint mirror the human auditory-to-executive pathway: \textbf{Cochlea} decomposes stereo mixtures into four phase-coherent stems, \textbf{Tympanum} extracts $50$\,Hz metrical and vocal-breath structure, \textbf{Cortex} plans beat-synchronous stem trajectories, and \textbf{Cingulate} monitors $30$ DJ conflict and defect modes~\citep{botvinick2001conflict} to rerank candidate plans. We name the model family \textbf{\sone{}} after Stevens' psychoacoustic unit of perceived loudness~\citep{stevens1936scale}, reflecting our core empirical finding that club-grade transitions hinge on perceived loudness continuity, vocal-formant phase coherence, and sub-$2.2$\,ms beat synchronisation.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Four-tier unified neuro-symbolic DJ family (\S\ref{sec:method}):} \textbf{Sone-6.5M} ($6.52$\,M params, $13.12$\,MB FP16), \textbf{Sone-v2-Base} ($13.61$\,M, $27.36$\,MB), \textbf{Sone-Pro} ($26.37$\,M, $52.90$\,MB), and \textbf{Sone-Ultra} ($39.40$\,M, $79.0$\,MB), all sharing a single zero-copy Rust/WebAssembly wire format (\op{sone-infer/1}) and master-spliced boundary execution ($4.8\%$--$47.7\%$ of one Xeon vCPU per song).
  \item \textbf{Phase-coherent Dual-Axis \op{Cochlea} \& 2-stage \op{BS-RoFormer-1297} cascade (\S\ref{sec:stems}):} Scaling from $64$ vocal-harmonic bands (\op{Base/Pro}) to $83$ ultra-fine vocal-formant subbands (\op{Cochlea-Ultra}, $33.10$\,M params, $10$ blocks, $d=160$) with bounded complex Wiener projection ($\|\sum_m \hat{s}_m - x\|_\infty < -148$\,dB), silent-vocal pre-onset suppression, direct time-domain SDR loss, and a two-stage GPU teacher cascade (\op{BS-RoFormer-1297}~\citep{lu2024bsroformer} for $12.98$\,dB vocal extraction followed by \op{HTDemucs-ft}~\citep{rouard2023hybrid} on the exact vocal-free instrumental residual).
  \item \textbf{Vinyl-speed equivariant \op{Tympanum}, comb-grid \& Newton drum-slope lock (\S\ref{sec:ear}):} Exact pitch/key/tuning/tempo equivariant resampling ($\alpha = 2^{\Delta s/12}$) across $600$ stem-isolated and modern tracks ($0.865$ raw beat F1), fused with an onset-flux comb grid and closed-form joint Newton drum-slope + phase-nudge lock holding rendered beat drift within $\le 2.2$\,ms ($0.00$\,ms lag across $10/10$ studio mixes).
  \item \textbf{\op{Cortex v4} \& 12-mode counterfactual \op{Cingulate v4} from iterative blind listening (\S\ref{sec:beat}):} Resolving five subtle real-world pathologies exposed by blind 10-mix ear testing---live-drummer tempo drift, missed vocal overlap over instrumental outros, post-drop vocal/melody mutes, relative Camelot false clashes, and pre-vocal stem-bleed fuzz---cutting held-out timing MAE below $0.15$\,bars ($100\%$ owner recipe agreement across $106$ accepted mixes).
